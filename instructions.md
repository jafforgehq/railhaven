Build a mobile idle colony management game in Godot 4.
Working title: Ember Rail.

Use the attached mockups as visual references. Resource numbers,
costs and small UI differences are illustrative, not final requirements.

VISUALS AND CAMERA
- Portrait mobile layout.
- True 2D side-view cutaway train with single-deck carriages.
- Horizontal drag pans the camera along the train.
- Tap a carriage to open its management panel.
- Train movement is shown through scrolling parallax scenery,
  wheel animation and environmental effects.
- Dark armored exterior, warm orange wasteland, cool teal interiors.
- Workers and machinery visibly animate during automatic production.

PREMISE
Outside temperatures reach 300°C.
The train is a continuously moving settlement protecting survivors.
The player expands and improves this moving home.

CORE LOOP
Automatic drone salvage → scrap → workshop produces parts →
upgrade or build carriages → support more survivors →
increase production → meet requirements for the next region.

PLAYER ACTIONS
- Build and upgrade carriages.
- Assign workers to jobs.
- Improve production, storage and cooling capacity.
- Choose priorities during environmental events.
- Track chapter goals and unlock new regions.

AUTOMATION
Workers perform their jobs automatically.
Drones automatically repeat salvage missions.
Production continues while the app is closed, subject to storage limits.
Offline rewards must account for production and consumption.
Automatic safe mode protects the crew when resources run low;
growth slows instead of causing offline deaths.

INITIAL CARRIAGES
- Living quarters: increases survivor capacity.
- Greenhouse: produces food.
- Water recycler: recovers usable water.
- Workshop: converts scrap into mechanical parts.
- Cooling module: increases supported train capacity.
- Drone bay: gathers scrap automatically.

MAIN SCREENS
Train, Crew and Journey.
Contextual panels handle construction and upgrades.

FIRST PLAYABLE SCOPE
One locomotive, modular carriages, one desert environment,
automatic workers, salvage, production, upgrades, local saving,
offline calculation and one chapter goal.

The first major goal is restoring a climate tower remotely using
drones and accumulated parts, unlocking the next region.

Prioritize a complete playable production-and-expansion loop.
Use placeholder assets first, while preserving the side-view layout.
